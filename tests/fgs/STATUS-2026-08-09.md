# Current FGS architecture and next gate — 2026-08-09

This is the current entry point for the FGS research record. It does not erase
the chronological findings; it separates results that still change a decision
from results that were corrected or retracted later. Read the cited document
before relying on a number.

## Branch and deployment boundary

| line | purpose | output-changing FGS state |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `master` | conservative production line, merged with upstream NVEnc 9.31 | bilateral separator, residual-fitted model; no PSD, `modelsrc`, motion prototype or response closure |
| `fgs/test-automation` | research and measurement line | source fitting plus several guarded prototypes; `modelsrc` remains default-off and the response/temporal paths require explicit test environment variables |

Do not merge the research branch wholesale. Relative to the merged 9.31 base it
adds about 4,700 lines and removes about 2,000 in `NVEncCore`, and contains debug
controls, rejected arms and experiment-only environment hooks alongside the
viable work. Promote a minimal component only after its independent gate passes.

## The architecture, layer by layer

1. **Admission and block selection.** Decide which blocks contain usable
   stochastic texture. This is not the same question as whether a model can fit
   that texture. Coverage, solver validity and semantic/stochastic evidence are
   separate states.
2. **Separation.** Bilateral denoising makes the clean base and residual.
   Production uses it because it is stable across ordinary and grain-bearing
   material. Motion separation can save substantially more bytes but remains a
   different base operator with disocclusion risk.
3. **Texture model.** Production fits AV1 AR coefficients from the denoiser
   residual. The residual is already whitened, which makes production grain too
   fine and can give lag-2 the wrong sign. `modelsrc=on` fits the AR texture from
   plane-detrended source blocks instead. This is the central architectural
   change and improves fine and coarse real-film texture by roughly an order of
   magnitude on the measured corpus.
4. **Strength and leak accounting.** Source fitting measures total source
   texture, while the encoded base retains some of it. Dense variance,
   realised-template normalization and post-encode leak work try to signal only
   the missing component. Whole-title amplitude is close; per-luma and chroma
   tails are not closed.
5. **Quantization and emission.** AV1 reduces the result to a compact, quantized
   scaling curve and AR table. Exact decoder replay has validated normative
   synthesis, but very coarse texture can still exceed what this representation
   reproduces exactly.
6. **Verification.** Energy, texture and base fidelity are deliberately
   separate measurements:

   - shared-source-mask `flat_retention.py` / `grain-watch.py` asks how much
     high-frequency energy survived;
   - lagged ACF and normalized spectra ask whether the texture is fine/coarse
     and spatially shaped correctly;
   - grain-disabled base comparison asks whether real picture detail was
     replaced by synthesized texture;
   - complete `libdav1d` decoding asks whether the stream is sound.

No one score answers all four questions. Finished-frame VMAF, SSIMULACRA2 and
Butteraugli penalize positionally different synthesized grain; use them on
grain-disabled, aligned bases for base fidelity, not to rank grain models.

## Findings that still hold

- The production analyzer is fast enough. The measured analyzer-kernel penalty
  was removed by the r4033/r4069 work; speed is not the quality blocker.
- Bilateral remains the conservative separator. Source fitting works with the
  same bilateral base, changes bytes by well under one percent in the isolated
  comparisons, and substantially improves delivered texture and amplitude.
- Source fitting is a better **model** on every measured positive and negative.
  Therefore model improvement cannot be used as admission evidence.
- Solver/coverage fallback is useful and implemented behind `modelsrc=on`.
  Rejected or insufficient source fits can fall back to the residual model.
- Covariance/texture-leak closure moves synthesized texture away from codec
  artifact and toward retained-original grain on the tested recompression
  pairs. It remains a guarded research path, not a production default.
- Production spatial retention is centred near the target, not uniformly
  wrong. After rebuilding exact source provenance, a clean five-scene
  population of 36 titles is median 1.046 (p10 0.873, p90 1.302). The
  provenance-correct temporal gate did not reproduce the declared upper tail
  as synthesis-amplitude excess. The spatial monitor remains a useful
  energy/destruction alarm, but retained base detail, ringing and texture mean
  it must not calibrate FGS gain. The earlier `n=22` values are invalid because
  their pair cache included wrong releases.

## Claims that must not drive another change

- The fitted `delivered ~= source^0.726` response came largely from a flawed
  investigation selector. Do not build a calibration from it.
- `emitted_vs_delivered.py` did not pass its consistency check. It cannot split
  analyzer intent from synthesis delivery.
- The 2.5x animation result used already-transcoded AV1 as its source and is
  retracted by `FINDINGS-2026-08-07-LIBRARY-AUDIT.md`.
- The fixed correlation/anisotropy conjunction detects properties of
  recompression and texture, but has not identified a case where FGS is proven
  worse than plain. It is not a release router.
- PSD shaping was a net loss on real film. Motion compression results do not
  authorize changing the separator while this sequence is quality-first.

## Completed gate: provenance-correct tails

The tail-first gate completed on seven retained originals and five arms. Six
titles had five of five gradable scenes; one coverage-limited Korra title was
kept as observation-only. All 35 streams had exactly 600 frames and passed a
complete dav1d decode.

Source fitting reduced luma amplitude MAE from 0.155 to 0.076 and texture MAE
from 0.132 to 0.081 at +0.072% bytes. The guarded response reached 0.072 and
0.065 at +0.093% bytes. Direct grain-disabled base comparisons were comparable
to candidate build drift. The architecture therefore has a real quality gain
with no meaningful size or base-quality cost.

It is not production-ready. Source fitting worsened occupancy-weighted
amplitude MAE in the darkest luma band from 0.083 to 0.124, and worsened chroma
V amplitude MAE from 0.117 to 0.127 even while improving V texture. The
response path did not close either failure. The result localizes the next work
to strength provenance rather than justifying another global correction.

These are the corrected `scene-grid-v3-ratio-of-means` values. The initially
published mean-of-per-frame-ratios result overstated the V regression as 42%;
the canonical increase is 8.6%. The old statistic and Jensen gap remain in the
raw reports for auditability; see
`FINDINGS-2026-08-09-TAIL-ARCHITECTURE.md`.

## Completed gate: texture/strength provenance split

The follow-up kept source-derived AR texture while independently selecting
source or residual strength statistics. It added a pinned no-hook control and
two guarded arms across the same seven reels; all 21 new streams had 600 frames
and passed complete dav1d decoding.

The split localized both open amplitude defects. Residual-derived strength on
all planes improved the darkest luma band from source fit's `1.115 / 0.124`
amplitude/MAE to `0.935 / 0.080`, but returned the much larger `0.125–0.250`
band from `1.022 / 0.036` to production-like `0.790 / 0.210`. Source strength
therefore remains clearly better outside the darkest population; a fixed
source or residual choice cannot close the luma curve.

Residual V strength also fails as a universal chroma fix. It changes V from
`1.068 / 0.127` to `0.890 / 0.124`, compared with production's
`0.896 / 0.117`. It swaps mild over-delivery for mild under-delivery, remains
worse than production amplitude MAE, and gives back some of the source texture
gain. The safer choice reverses by title, so the next estimator must follow
temporal/content evidence rather than plane identity.

The gate exposed two architectural/test constraints. The anti-twinkle state
holds one combined parameter object, so changing strength changes emitted AR
texture on 29.2% of frames in the all-residual arm and 2.6% in the V-only arm.
It also caught independent run-to-run variation at an analyzer curve boundary
and in NVENC's base encode. Both pinned binaries produced the analyzer variants,
while exact repeats cleared the new refactor as their direct cause; base
variation was observed independently. Exact controls can be obtained, but
future small-effect gates must quantify repeat variance rather than assume one
encode is an oracle.

See `FINDINGS-2026-08-09-STRENGTH-PROVENANCE.md` for the corpus, per-title,
per-luma, base-delta, texture-isolation and control-repeat evidence.

## What remains open

1. **Temporal texture/strength coupling.** The solver can select strength
   provenance independently, but the combined anti-twinkle history can change
   which AR snapshot is emitted when a strength curve changes.
2. **Run-to-run stability.** Floating analyzer reduction/simplification and
   NVENC base decisions can vary independently. Their frequency and perceptual
   magnitude are not yet bounded.
3. **Per-luma amplitude.** Source-derived strength strongly improves the
   0.125–0.250 band but over-delivers the populated 0.000–0.125 band; residual
   strength has the opposite error.
4. **Chroma V amplitude.** Neither fixed source nor residual V strength is
   uniformly safe. U and V cannot share an aggregate verdict, and the surviving
   evidence points to temporal/saturation conditioning rather than a V gain.
5. **Admission.** This grain-bearing corpus did not produce a quality-labelled
   case where every FGS arm loses to plain AV1.
6. **Coarse-model representability.** A correct source observation can still be
   approximated imperfectly by AV1's compact quantized AR model.

## Next gate: make the strength estimator genuinely independent

Keep the bilateral separator, source AR model and cached source reels fixed.
Proceed in this order:

1. split the temporal hold/update history so strength changes cannot select a
   different AR snapshot;
2. make the control gate repeat-aware and report analyzer-table and base-encode
   variance without silently rerolling failures;
3. prototype a continuous per-luma strength estimator offline, using source
   strength outside the darkest population and measured source/base confidence
   inside it; validate leave-one-title-out rather than hardcoding `0.125`;
4. prototype chroma amplitude from temporal and saturation evidence, keeping U
   and V separate and rejecting any whole-plane constant;
5. run the exact corpus, summed bytes, emitted-texture audit, direct
   grain-disabled base deltas and complete dav1d validation again.

Do not apply the response closure, add a global gain, change routing or promote
`modelsrc` before those gates pass. The fixed residual-strength arms have now
answered their localization question and should remain test-only controls, not
shipping candidates.
