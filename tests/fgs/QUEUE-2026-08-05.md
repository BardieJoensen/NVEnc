## Queued

1. **FGS content gate (production).** `--av1-film-grain` is applied by a single
   unconditional encode template, so grain-free content gets ~1.9x synthesized
   grain it never had. A source-sigma threshold in the flow fixes this without
   touching the encoder or waiting on the measurement work. Highest-value
   remaining production item.
2. **Bucket calibration for animation / clean digital.** They now carry a
   film-derived qvbr calibration (28/33/37) that was measured only on three
   grain-heavy 4K films.
3. **Does `arGain` explain which titles over-deliver?** The emission exponent
   is heterogeneous across titles (`0.40`--`1.46` among well-determined fits,
   `FINDINGS-2026-08-06-EMISSION-EXPONENT.md`), which rules out a fixed response
   curve in the per-bin strength fit. For luma `templateGain = arGain`, a
   variance ratio describing correlation structure rather than amplitude, so it
   is free to vary per title. Recover it from the emitted AR coefficients and
   regress over-delivery on it. Tables are already on disk.
4. **Find the defect in the estimator.** Both floors are exonerated
   (`FINDINGS-2026-08-06-FLOOR-ABLATION.md`): the denoise clamp by its ~1.1x
   sensitivity, the selection floor by not binding at all. The one-defect
   relationship is therefore a property of what the analyser does with the
   blocks it admits. Re-read the six rejected estimators as attempts on that,
   now that "the floor was interfering" is not available as an excuse.
5. **Is the decoupling scene-driven?** Map emitted table intervals onto frames
   (ticks are 1e-7 s; a 288-frame 23.976 clip spans 120,120,000) and test
   whether over-delivery concentrates where the model holds an interval across
   a real change in source grain. Shining V has two intervals at mean 51--53
   against a 8--22 baseline, so the data to check is already on disk.
6. **Densify the two remaining HEVC cells** (Taxi 15000k, Interstellar 15000k)
   to 16 pairs for symmetry with the 6000k cells that were densified.
7. **Chroma texture at 16 pairs.** `FINDINGS-2026-08-05-CHROMA-DIAGNOSIS.md`
   quotes lag-1 `0.020` / lag-2 `0.039` from six pairs. Pooled axes are stable
   at six, but confirming on the three planes that matter costs little.
