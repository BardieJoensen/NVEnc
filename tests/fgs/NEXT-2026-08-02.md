# Where `modelsrc` stands and what is left, 2026-08-02

Written against `b41df746`. Nothing here has been implemented. This is a plan
plus one new analysis of numbers already in
`FINDINGS-2026-08-01-SOURCE-FIT.md`; where it disagrees with an earlier
conclusion that is called out explicitly.

## Settled, do not re-litigate

- **Source fitting is right and materially better.** Mean lag-1 grain-texture
  error 0.192 -> 0.056, improving all six titles. The AV1 model is not the
  limit; libaom expresses Taxi's grain from the same residual.
- **The compression target is met.** Motion separation saves 46.4% across six
  4K films against plain encoding, bilateral 23.1%, against a 30-40% target.
- **The correlation regulariser is correct.** It removes only genuinely
  over-correlated intervals.
- **Both `modelsrc=off` and `=on` pass all 22 GPU fixtures**, and `=off` is
  byte-identical to the pre-change encoder.
- **Rejected and still rejected:** residual-derived strength (0.669), flat-mask
  swaps (Deer improves, Taxi reverses), post-hoc amplitude multipliers, and a
  single constant attenuation for base leakage.

## The error budget, re-differenced

Amplitudes relative to source temporal truth, from the six-film closure table.
`true post target` is by construction the synthesis that would make played
total exactly 1.000.

| difference | mean | range | consistent sign? |
| --- | ---: | --- | --- |
| delivered - **true** post target | **-0.085** | -0.154 .. -0.013 | **yes, all six short** |
| spatial estimate - true post target | -0.076 | -0.118 .. -0.034 | yes, all six low |
| temporal estimate - true post target | -0.053 | -0.073 .. -0.023 | yes, all six low |
| delivered - spatial estimate | -0.009 | -0.096 .. +0.077 | no |

**This reorders the problem.** The findings observed that delivered synthesis
"cannot be inferred from the spatial curve alone" and scatters +/-0.1 around it
with no consistent sign. True — but that scatter is nearly zero-mean. Measured
against the *correct* target instead, every title is short, and the deficit
splits cleanly:

- **a systematic estimation bias of -0.076** (spatial) or **-0.053**
  (temporal), which is the whole reason played total averages 0.916;
- **a zero-mean emission scatter of +/-0.09**, which does not bias the corpus
  but does mean no single title's delivery can be predicted from its curve.

Two different problems with two different fixes. The systematic term is where
the missing amplitude is; the scatter term is why per-title validation is noisy.

## New: the base-leak spread is amplitude, not title identity

The findings rejected a fixed temporal correction because NVENC preserves
30.1%-77.5% of pre-encode base residue, a 2.57x spread, and because luma
banding does not collapse it. Both observations hold. But the spread is not
unexplained variance:

**Pre-encode leak versus the fraction NVENC preserves: r = 0.928.** Bigger base
residue survives the encoder better, monotonically. Fitting `post` against
`pre` gives a slope of **1.037** with a negative offset — which is the
signature of a quantiser deadzone, not a gain. Amplitudes below the deadzone
are destroyed, amplitudes above it pass nearly intact.

Testing that form against the alternatives, leave-one-title-out:

| model | params | LOO max | LOO mean |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| `post = max(0, pre - theta)`, theta = 0.159 | **1** | **0.0437** | **0.0242** |
| `post = m*pre + c` | 2 | 0.0683 | 0.0344 |
| `post = g*pre` (constant gain) | 1 | 0.1448 | 0.0472 |

The rejected model was constant *gain*, and rejecting it was right — its
leave-one-out error is 0.145, worse than the error being chased. A constant
*deadzone* is a different model, has the same single parameter, generalises
three times better, and has a physical reason to exist.

**This does not make it usable yet.** LOO max 0.044 is still half the deficit,
it is six points at one QVBR, and `theta` must scale with the quantiser step or
it is a corpus average wearing physics as a costume. Which is exactly what the
next experiment tests.

## Plan

### Phase 0 — QVBR sweep against the deadzone (decisive, no encoder change)

Re-run the existing six-film closure measurement at three or four QVBR values
instead of one, and fit `theta` at each.

- **If `theta` scales with the quantiser step**, this is a transfer function.
  It is one parameter the encoder already knows at analysis time, and the
  systematic -0.053 becomes correctable in a principled way.
- **If `theta` is flat or unordered in QP**, the relationship is a corpus
  coincidence at one rate, and temporal leak compensation should be abandoned
  as an encoder input exactly as the findings concluded.

This is the single highest-information experiment available and it needs no
CUDA. It uses `strength_selection_report.py` and the closure harness as they
already exist.

**Cost:** six titles x four rates of encode-plus-double-decode.

### Phase 1 — emission audit (offline, deterministic, method already validated)

The +/-0.09 scatter between the signalled curve and delivered synthesis is
unexplained, and it is the reason no per-title result is trustworthy to better
than 0.1. It is also fully closed-loop: given a table, what the decoder
produces is deterministic.

Predict synthesis amplitude from each of the six tables alone — curve value per
luma bin, realised template std from the spec's AR recursion, `scaling_shift`,
`grain_scale_shift` — weighted by the *actual luma histogram of the measured
blocks*, and compare against delivered. `ar_acf.py` already does this and
validated at 0.997 on white grain, which is how the template clipping bug was
found.

Two outcomes, both useful:

- **prediction matches delivery** -> emission is understood, and the scatter is
  the strength *curve* being wrong per luma bin. That points at curve
  population: the bins where grain is measured are not the bins where it is
  played. Directly checkable against the same histograms.
- **prediction does not match delivery** -> there is a second amplitude bug of
  the same kind as the clipping one, and it is worth more than any estimation
  work.

Run Phase 1 **before** implementing anything from Phase 0. A leak correction
validated through a +/-0.09 measurement lens cannot be evaluated honestly.

### Phase 2 — implement, only if Phase 0 holds

Feed the leak estimate into the **existing** `sqrt(1 - retain^2)` closure rather
than adding a second variance-closure path. Measured retention and intentional
retention are the same arithmetic; two paths can disagree.

Open design question to settle from Phase 0's coverage numbers, not by
assumption: static-block temporal estimation with spatial fallback, versus
motion-aligned differencing using the degrain analysis's existing vectors and
SAD confidence. The latter has better coverage but is only available under
`denoiser=motion`.

### Phase 3 — the motion perceptual gate (independent, already prepared)

`FINDINGS-2026-08-02-MOTION-REVIEW.md`. Blinded base and finished pairs for The
Shining, The Deer Hunter and Scarface. Not blocked by any of the above and
should not wait for it — it decides whether the 46.4% arm exists at all, which
changes how much the amplitude work is worth.

## Current implementations that deserve another look

- **Rectification bias in the strength subtraction.** `variance = fmax(0.0,
  V_source - V_base)` clamps at zero per block. With the wrong quantity being
  subtracted, some blocks clamp and contribute a hard zero to `binVarSum` while
  still incrementing `binBlockCount`, which drags the bin mean down. Nothing has
  counted how many blocks clamp. If it is more than a few percent on any title
  this is a live bias, and it is one cheap counter to find out.
- **Chroma is unvalidated on real film.** Every corpus number above is luma.
  Chroma runs the same source fit and the same base subtraction, and the KAT's
  `chroma_corr`/`chroma_ind` fixtures pass, but no real-film chroma grain
  fidelity has been measured under `modelsrc=on` at all.
- **Keep as-is:** dense 1024-pixel strength (0.837 -> 0.841) and generalised
  realised template gain (0.841 -> 0.852/0.865). Both small, both measured
  safe, both correct in principle. Neither is the remaining loss, and neither
  should be reverted to buy simplicity.

## A question that is not technical

Variance closure is currently aiming at played total = 1.000. That treats grain
surviving in the base as equivalent to synthesised grain. It is not: base
residue has been through the encoder, and the plain-encode rows show what the
codec leaves behind is *more* correlated than film grain (lag-1 0.84-0.86
against a source 0.80) because it is partly ringing rather than grain.

So closing to 1.000 by energy may be signalling slightly too little real grain
in exchange for codec artifact. Played total currently averages 0.916 with
substantially better texture than before. Whether 0.916-with-correct-texture
beats 1.000-with-mixed-texture is a perceptual question, and the review set in
Phase 3 is already the right instrument to answer it — worth judging
deliberately rather than assuming 1.000 is the target.

## Status

`modelsrc` stays default-off. Nothing deployed to Tdarr. The motion arm is not
a production candidate until the perceptual review passes.
