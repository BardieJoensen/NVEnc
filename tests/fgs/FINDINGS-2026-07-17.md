# AV1 film-grain analyzer audit — 2026-07-17

## Result

The branch now has a materially better and more stable CUDA film-grain
analyzer.  It preserves more structured detail, offers opt-in adaptive
residual retention, resets the model at shot changes, and avoids rewriting a
slightly different grain model almost every frame.

This work improves quality and AV1 film-grain signaling; it is not an encoder
throughput optimization.  Across the six short real-title controls, total
encode time was within 1% of plain NVENC, which is inside the noise of such
short runs.  The useful speed/space effect for a Tdarr flow is therefore lower
bitrate on suitable grainy material, not faster transcoding.

Comparison boundary:

- Synthetic benchmark before: `65749ce6e606` (`baseline-fft3d`).
- Reference-analyzer baseline: `46ccff44c66b`, pinned libaom revision
  `18c52422b835ba6cdde1b2342d760c6037a7fd86`.
- Detail-separator control immediately before the coherence change:
  `4583c2311694`.
- Optimized build: `93ae7663a0aa`.
- GPU: NVIDIA GeForce RTX 5060 Ti, driver 595.71.05.

The checked-in generated benchmark contains the exact repository state,
NVEncC hash, GPU and tool versions, commands, logs, KAT metrics, and retention
sweep.

## What changed

1. The FFT3D clean-base refinement now measures local gradient covariance and
   fades denoising out over directionally coherent blocks.  Lines, edges, and
   patterned texture are less likely to be classified as random grain.
2. `retain=auto` is an explicit quality-first mode.  It converts the rolling
   structured-detail risk into 0.00–0.50 residual retention, applies a
   deadband, and reduces synthesized variance so retained plus regenerated
   grain stays approximately constant.  The existing numeric/default behavior
   is unchanged.
3. A spatial block-mean detector resets history even for equal-grain shot
   changes.  New model fits must then persist for three frames and are normally
   accepted no more often than once per 24 frames; a detected cut accepts its
   first model immediately.
4. The campaign records analyzer risk/retention and uses CUDA implementations
   for the expensive full-reference metric features.

Adaptive retention behaves differently on known content classes:

- Flat isotropic grain: risk 0.003, retention 0.00.
- Fine structured detail plus grain: risk 0.332, retention 0.50.
- Fine-detail transfer with auto retention: 0.669.
- Systematic structured-edge bias with auto retention: 1.00 8-bit code value
  RMS.

The fixed-retention controls did not regress.  Played-out grain amplitude is
unchanged to three decimal places at every tested 8/10-bit retention level;
encoded-byte movement is between -0.16% and +0.13%.

The heavy-grain SSIMULACRA2 exception needs context: decoder-generated grain
does not occupy the source grain's original pixel positions.  A full-reference
metric can penalize a correct new random realization.  The complementary
high-frequency sigma moved from 1.60 to 3.65 against a source value of 3.13,
so grain energy became substantially more faithful even though pixel-aligned
SSIMULACRA2 fell.

## Reference implementations

The implementation was guided by, but does not copy, three upstream designs:

- [libaom `noise_model`](https://aomedia.googlesource.com/aom/+/refs/heads/main/examples/noise_model.c)
  explicitly consumes an original and a denoised stream.  Using it twice let
  the tests separate clean-base errors from model-fitting errors.
- [SVT-AV1's film-grain description](https://gitlab.com/AOMediaCodec/SVT-AV1/-/raw/master/Docs/Appendix-Film-Grain-Synthesis.md)
  documents flat-area selection, Wiener denoising, AR fitting, and specifically
  notes that temporal smoothing could improve parameter signaling.
- [VVenC's film-grain analyzer](https://github.com/fraunhoferhhi/vvenc/blob/master/source/Lib/EncoderLib/SEIFilmGrainAnalyzer.cpp)
  uses gradient orientation, Canny edges, morphological expansion, and
  multi-resolution masks to exclude structure.  That reinforced the choice to
  add a directional-coherence signal rather than only tuning denoiser strength.

The pinned libaom comparison says the ordinary white/detail/chroma model fits
are already close (roughly 0.985–1.053 of libaom synthesis amplitude).  Coarse
correlated grain is still weak: NVEnc reproduces 35.7% of source sigma while
libaom with an ideal clean base reproduces 46.3%.  This is partly analyzer loss
and partly the compact AV1 AR grain model's representational limit.

## Metric backend

- VMAF: CUDA ADM, VIF, and motion feature extraction selected with
  `--gpumask 0`; the small final VMAF model prediction is CPU.
- PSNR, SSIM, CIEDE2000: libvmaf `psnr_cuda`, `ssim_cuda`, and `ciede_cuda`.
- SSIMULACRA2: FFVship CUDA.

This is therefore a CUDA-scored campaign, not a claim that every scalar or
model-evaluation instruction runs on the GPU.

## Recommendation and remaining limits

For a quality-first trial:

```text
--av1-film-grain denoise=auto,chroma=auto,denoiser=fft3d,retain=auto
```

Keep it opt-in in Tdarr and first target visibly grainy sources.  It is not yet
a good blanket setting for every library: five of the six real controls reached
the current 0.50 retention cap, showing that the first-generation risk mapping
is conservative and not yet discriminating enough between film texture,
animation edges, and true grain.

The natural next work is a larger scene-labeled calibration corpus, a
multi-scale temporal grain/detail classifier, a temporal flicker metric, and a
time-based (rather than fixed-frame) model cadence.  Coarse correlated grain
also needs a dedicated investigation, but its libaom result shows that software
alone cannot make the compact AV1 synthesis model reproduce every grain stock.
