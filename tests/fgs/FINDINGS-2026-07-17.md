# AV1 film-grain analyzer audit — 2026-07-17

## Result

> **Superseded Taxi Driver conclusion (2026-07-29):** `09dae08c` found that
> the sparse analyzer's fixed 8x8 sampling lattice aliased Taxi's residual and
> inflated AR gain, halving the signalled strength. A staggered sampler raises
> four-scene grain retention from 0.61-0.76 to 0.83-0.92 while reducing the
> matched QVBR 26 files by 22-32%; its decoded grain matches libaom within
> -6.6% to +3.3%. See `FINDINGS-2026-07-29-PERFORMANCE.md`. The routing result
> below remains a record of the old binary, not a current recommendation.

The branch now has a materially better and more stable CUDA film-grain
analyzer.  It preserves more structured detail, offers opt-in adaptive
residual retention, resets the model at shot changes, and avoids rewriting a
slightly different grain model almost every frame.

This work improves quality and AV1 film-grain signaling; it is not an encoder
throughput optimization.  Across the six short real-title controls, total
encode time was within 1% of plain NVENC, which is inside the noise of such
short runs.  The useful speed/space effect for a Tdarr flow is therefore lower
bitrate on suitable grainy material, not faster transcoding.

Comparison boundary:

- Synthetic benchmark before: `65749ce6e606` (`baseline-fft3d`).
- Reference-analyzer baseline: `46ccff44c66b`, pinned libaom revision
  `18c52422b835ba6cdde1b2342d760c6037a7fd86`.
- Detail-separator control immediately before the coherence change:
  `4583c2311694`.
- Optimized build: `93ae7663a0aa`.
- GPU: NVIDIA GeForce RTX 5060 Ti, driver 595.71.05.

The checked-in generated benchmark contains the exact repository state,
NVEncC hash, GPU and tool versions, commands, logs, KAT metrics, and retention
sweep.

## What changed

1. The FFT3D clean-base refinement now measures local gradient covariance and
   fades denoising out over directionally coherent blocks.  Lines, edges, and
   patterned texture are less likely to be classified as random grain.
2. `retain=auto` is an explicit quality-first mode.  It converts the rolling
   structured-detail risk into 0.00–0.50 residual retention, applies a
   deadband, and reduces synthesized variance so retained plus regenerated
   grain stays approximately constant.  The existing numeric/default behavior
   is unchanged.
3. A spatial block-mean detector resets history even for equal-grain shot
   changes.  New model fits must then persist for three frames and are normally
   accepted no more often than once per 24 frames; a detected cut accepts its
   first model immediately.
4. The campaign records analyzer risk/retention and uses CUDA implementations
   for the expensive full-reference metric features.

The heavy-grain SSIMULACRA2 exception needs context: decoder-generated grain
does not occupy the source grain's original pixel positions.  A full-reference
metric can penalize a correct new random realization.  The complementary
high-frequency sigma moved from 1.60 to 3.65 against a source value of 3.13,
so grain energy became substantially more faithful even though pixel-aligned
SSIMULACRA2 fell.

That HF reading is necessary but not sufficient, and the matched-bitrate
addendum below shows it overstated the case for heavy grain: energy can land
on target while the grain's *size* does not.

## Reference implementations

The implementation was guided by, but does not copy, three upstream designs:

- [libaom `noise_model`](https://aomedia.googlesource.com/aom/+/refs/heads/main/examples/noise_model.c)
  explicitly consumes an original and a denoised stream.  Using it twice let
  the tests separate clean-base errors from model-fitting errors.
- [SVT-AV1's film-grain description](https://gitlab.com/AOMediaCodec/SVT-AV1/-/raw/master/Docs/Appendix-Film-Grain-Synthesis.md)
  documents flat-area selection, Wiener denoising, AR fitting, and specifically
  notes that temporal smoothing could improve parameter signaling.
- [VVenC's film-grain analyzer](https://github.com/fraunhoferhhi/vvenc/blob/master/source/Lib/EncoderLib/SEIFilmGrainAnalyzer.cpp)
  uses gradient orientation, Canny edges, morphological expansion, and
  multi-resolution masks to exclude structure.  That reinforced the choice to
  add a directional-coherence signal rather than only tuning denoiser strength.

The pinned libaom comparison says the ordinary white/detail/chroma model fits
are already close (roughly 0.985–1.053 of libaom synthesis amplitude).  Coarse
correlated grain is still weak: NVEnc reproduces 35.7% of source sigma while
libaom with an ideal clean base reproduces 46.3%.  This is partly analyzer loss
and partly the compact AV1 AR grain model's representational limit.

## Metric backend

- VMAF: CUDA ADM, VIF, and motion feature extraction selected with
  `--gpumask 0`; the small final VMAF model prediction is CPU.
- PSNR, SSIM, CIEDE2000: libvmaf `psnr_cuda`, `ssim_cuda`, and `ciede_cuda`.
- SSIMULACRA2: FFVship CUDA.

This is therefore a CUDA-scored campaign, not a claim that every scalar or
model-evaluation instruction runs on the GPU.

## Recommendation and remaining limits

Production update, 2026-07-29: the generated controls below do not establish a
production denoiser ranking. A real Silo S03E01/E02 QVBR 29 comparison favored
bilateral over FFT3D on SSIMULACRA2 mean/p5, Butteraugli, and grain retention.
Keep the production-tested bilateral setting pending a broader real-title A/B.
The historical trial setting was:

```text
--av1-film-grain denoise=auto,chroma=auto,denoiser=fft3d,retain=auto
```

Keep it opt-in in Tdarr and first target visibly grainy sources.  It is not yet
a good blanket setting for every library: five of the six real controls reached
the current 0.50 retention cap, showing that the first-generation risk mapping
is conservative and not yet discriminating enough between film texture,
animation edges, and true grain.

Two qualifications from the matched-bitrate addendum below, which was measured
after this section was written:

- "First target visibly grainy sources" is too broad.  Target sources whose
  grain the AR model can represent — fine grain.  The coarsest 35 mm stock is
  the worst case for this feature, not the best, and is better served by a plain
  tuned encode.
- The cap, not the risk mapping, is what flattens the discrimination.

The natural next work is a larger scene-labeled calibration corpus, a
multi-scale temporal grain/detail classifier, a temporal flicker metric, and a
time-based (rather than fixed-frame) model cadence.  Coarse correlated grain
also needs a dedicated investigation, but its libaom result shows that software
alone cannot make the compact AV1 synthesis model reproduce every grain stock.

the question the library actually poses: at an equal byte budget on heavy 35 mm
grain, are the bits better spent on synthesis metadata plus a cleaned base, or

--max-bitrate 64000` on the same 60 s 4K PQ clip, scored against `tx-ref600`

file.  Reproduce with `tests/fgs/matched_rate_sweep.py`.

Three findings, in order of importance.

**1. Grain energy was landing on target with the wrong grain size.**  HF sigma
alone looked like a win for synthesis (3.67 vs a 3.13 source, while plain and
SVT shed a third of the grain at 2.05–2.10).  The residual autocorrelation says
otherwise: the source's coarse 35 mm grain holds neighbouring samples at 0.367,
while zero-retention synthesis decorrelates at 0.186 — roughly half.  The
synthesized field carries slightly more energy than the source in a much finer
structure, i.e. sand rather than grain.  This is the real-content confirmation
of the 36% coarse-capture KAT metric, and it means the full-reference penalty
on this title is not only the position-randomness artifact; the texture genuinely
differs.  Retention pulls the structure back toward the source (0.186 → 0.249 at
alpha 0.60) precisely because it re-introduces real grain.

**2. Full-reference metrics cannot arbitrate this, and the monotonic retention
trend is partly their bias.**  VMAF rises monotonically with retention because
retention raises position correlation (0.08 → 0.98 in the synthetic sweep),
which is exactly what these metrics reward; the limit of the trend is plain
encoding.  The trend is therefore not evidence that retention is perceptually
better — it is evidence that alpha interpolates toward the metrics' preferred
answer.  Only the grain descriptors and eventually eyes can settle it.

**3. Plain tuned NVENC is the strongest option on this content at this size,
and beats SVT-AV1.**  86.84 / 81.22 against SVT crf30's 84.70 / 79.95 at a
slightly larger file, and ahead of every synthesis variant.  Its weakness is
visible only in the grain columns (HF 2.10, acf 0.538 — smoothed, and what
survives is blurrier than the source).  Note also that a plain tuned qvbr26
encode reached VMAF 82.3 at 145 MB, so on this title plain buys more quality per
byte than synthesis does at 250 MB.

### Routing conclusion

On this sampled Taxi Driver clip, plain tuned encoding should be preferred to
film-grain synthesis.  The compact AV1 AR model could not represent that
sample's grain size — the pinned libaom comparison already showed the ceiling
is partly the format, not the analyzer — so synthesis returned correct energy
in the wrong texture while giving up rate-quality against a plain encode. The
earlier "SVT is better on heavy grain" conclusion should also be retired for
this sample: the comparison was against FGS, and plain tuned NVENC beat SVT.

This single result does **not** establish a title list or a source-correlation
threshold. Subsequent measurements on six real 4K remasters span lag-one
correlation 0.33-0.82 continuously, with four high-correlation titles retaining
grain well under FGS. Source correlation is a descriptor, not a standalone
router.

The value case for this feature remains where the AR model fits the grain:
the fine digital grain 4K control (38% smaller than plain for 1.56 VMAF), and
that is what a Tdarr trial should target.  `retain=auto` remains a reasonable
quality-first default within that class.

### Auto-retention cap

The risk mapping discriminates better than the "not discriminating enough" note
above suggests; what erases the discrimination is the 0.50 cap.  Measured block
coherence (structure-tensor anisotropy, replicated offline and confirmed against
the analyzer's own `risk=` log):

| clip | median coherence | blocks above HIGH (0.18) | mean risk | auto retain |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Taxi (heavy 35 mm 4K) | 0.093 | 13.6% | 0.26 | 0.40 |
| Batman (1080p) | 0.616 | 88.9% | 0.92 | 0.50 (capped) |

The `[0.12, 0.18]` window sits sensibly at Taxi's 65th–85th percentile, and the
0.26 vs 0.92 spread is a real separation.  But Batman's risk asks for 1.38
retention and is clamped to 0.50, so content that should disengage synthesis
almost entirely is held at a half measure instead.  Since `retain` -> 1.0
converges exactly to a plain encode (the synthesis scale goes to zero), lifting
the cap would let `retain=auto` act as the engage-gate the campaign asked for
rather than needing a separate one.  This is not yet a recommendation: on capped
titles auto currently costs only ~0.5 VMAF for ~10% fewer bytes, so the change
needs its own matched-rate test on capped content before the constant moves.
