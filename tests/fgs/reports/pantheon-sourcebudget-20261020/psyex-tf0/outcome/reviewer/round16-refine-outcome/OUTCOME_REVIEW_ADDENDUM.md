# R16 independent-review provenance addendum

This addendum corrects the support-verification scope stated in `OUTCOME_REVIEW.md` SHA `1cb9a6951aa15bac52eb05b95be55b6d14daaddc0c587b71cbf3a91a55293d33`.

The original `verify_support.py` SHA `e6b87f3b14f82d7f65ecd709695b86b19cb42c388a279350243a9aa9bf0b13bb` independently checked population counts and aggregate SSE closure, but it summed the parent's already-grouped SSE. It could not detect a spatially misassigned group whose totals still closed, and it lacked frozen-input/self/argv execution receipts. Its result is preserved as superseded supporting evidence.

The accepted replacement is `verify_support_v2.py` SHA `7d668d4b98cdbae21f19897aab6e4a0e53ee4220c7870356545bc4573daed1bc`. It pins the admission review, stage result, support result and admitted receipt; rebuilds nonoverlapping group masks from all 2,870 admitted token geometries; loads the admitted original, preloop and postloop surfaces directly; and independently recomputes per-group native preloop/postloop SSE. It also checks full ROI coverage, group units/pixels/qindex distributions, final-to-preloop aggregate equality, raw/scalar/final aggregate closure, before/after identities, its own identity, argv and non-optimized Python.

The replacement completed under outer receipt `support-review-v2-execution.json` SHA `0bf6cacb1c41dc5e5f19db9632ff65384d4d003ef2406f53d6f4d10afde941ae` and inner receipt `support-review-v2-receipt.json` SHA `f9e970e977fd60523816fbe6c0d391e5e4b91a3bdf8882acc411fb511dcb5bc8`. Its result SHA is `f77732332f4c0c163d1562e4940e1d5205a56ce57dcb63eec438475eb8d5e324`. All independent spatial comparisons passed exactly. Raw/scalar surfaces remain aggregate-only checks because they are not separate admitted surface files; this limitation does not affect the independently verified positive-EOB/uninvoked spatial attribution of admitted preloop and postloop error.
