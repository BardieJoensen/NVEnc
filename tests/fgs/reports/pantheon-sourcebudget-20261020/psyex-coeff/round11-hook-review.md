2. The post-`svt_aom_move_blk_data` commit check compares qindex, EOB, and TX type, but omits `mds_idx`, even though the production copy explicitly assigns it (`product_coding_loop.c:7206`). This makes the committed destination's owner identity direct rather than relying only on the retained source pointer and packed coefficient offset. `EcBlkStruct` has no `skip_mode`, `block_has_coeff`, or `y_has_coeff` members (`coding_unit.h:272-310`); those values in the commit record must be described as final source-owner facts, not fields proven copied into entropy metadata. ## Accepted implementation properties

- The hooks capture the final EncDec invocation, not an MD-search aggregate: raw coefficients are taken after the production forward transform and the token is bound inside the actual luma encode-pass quantizer call (`coding_loop.c:407-438`; `full_loop.c:1724-1840`). The first FP/B result, fast optimization, B requantization, `optimize_b`, noise-normalization return, and final arrays occur at their real boundaries. A recorded branch stage means the routine ran; equality of adjacent arrays is needed to call it a no-op.
- The adjusted coefficient domain is correct for reduced 64-point transforms: `n=av1_get_max_eob(tx)` and `n=min(w,32)*min(h,32)` are asserted, while the packed SB offset advances by spatial `w*h`. Full q/DQ arrays, scan/iscan, QM/IQM, tables, raw transform and native inverse snapshots are retained. Admission should additionally enforce EOB/scan consistency and zero coefficients beyond EOB for every stage.
- The private inverse uses thread-local aligned copies of predictor, DQ, and destination. It does not pass a live encoder output buffer to the inverse kernel. The final private inverse must exactly equal the production TU reconstruction; EOB0/skip explicitly use prediction and do not read stale coefficients.
- `pc_skip` covers syntax skip-mode paths that do not enter the normal encode loop; normal EncDec `md_skip` is already marked by `pc_begin`. These are distinct final-owner facts. Neither alone proves that forward quantization did or did not execute in search.
- The owner token retains the final block pointer, TU index, packed offset, geometry and deep-copied final qcoeff. The commit walk follows the production spatial packing and compares positive-EOB qcoeff against `quantized_coeff[sb]`. The accepted-attempt barrier requires all 510 pre-dispatch routes and started=ended=committed; rejected generations reset the owner maps.
- Trace records are serialized under `pc_lock`, including headers and payloads. The boundary observer acquires its own lock before calling `pc_attempt`/`pc_finish`, but coefficient code never acquires the boundary lock, so lock order is one-way. The coefficient FD is separately validated as append/write, regular, empty, and a different inode, with a 1 GiB cap and fatal write/fsync/close checks.
- With `SVT_PANTHEON_COEFF_FD` absent, the added calls return before touching codec state. With tracing enabled, the only computation beyond serialization is on private buffers. Off/on exact IVF and all reconstructed-frame identity are still required because this source-level reasoning does not prove bit neutrality.
