# Independent review and corrections

The same experiment worker and independent reviewer were reused. They were
given bounded contracts, separate ownership, no recursive delegation and no
production or Git-index authority. Parent retained experimental decisions,
scoring, integration and conclusions.

Before encoding:

- The worker proposed another variance-boost experiment. Parent rejected it
  for this round: it would revisit allocation before testing the distinct
  search/decision-policy uncertainty. Preset0 was selected once, with preset4
  and all other external settings fixed.
- The reviewer initially cited NVENC A-base9.648396Mbps as the SVT baseline.
  Parent required the actual B command/IVF/MKV/metric chain. Correct B scored
  rate is9.6763312Mbps; its IVF payload is9.6767152Mbps. Both are documented.
- The worker's first Y4M parser expected colon-separated width/height fields.
  The actual header usesW1920/H1080. Correct parsing was checked against the
  new fixture before encoding.
- Parent required effective-preset log checks, complete reconstruction shape,
  explicit packet cadence, scored-MKV rate accounting, executed-script copies
  and binding to the full336 historical input. Requested settings alone do
  not prove an intervention occurred.
- Reviewer flagged that an early `encoded` status could survive failed
  post-validation. Final runner now uses `validating` until all checks finish.
  The already running arm retains its exact earlier script; the scorer's
  independent complete-receipt/hash/rate checks prevent false admission.
- Reviewer required paired whole-window controls with the frozen outside mask,
  MSE p95 and signed quiet-HF tails. Parent implemented these and explicitly
  labeled the mask's historical scope.
- Reviewer recommended binding live validity and full-input receipts to their
  encode-time/historical hashes. Parent added both checks.
- The summary script initially trusted same-tag upstream files without all
  cross-links. Reviewer required command/assessment/metric/texture/live-output
  binding and raw-control provenance. Parent added those assertions, corrected
  “complete controls” to “control summaries,” linked full evidence and moved
  output-directory creation after validation.

The pre-score review found the corrected admission path consistent with the
contract. When the run landed below99%, parent separated practical eligibility
from matched-comparison eligibility in an explicit mode. Independent review
approved that distinction before quality results, with both rate ratios and
eligibility flags preserved. No texture/control/playback gate changed.

Outcome review accepted the completed preset0 arm as a valid failed under-cap
screen. It independently verified all336 decoder samples, complete source/hash/
settings identity, source/B rates and nonmatched labeling. The amplitude failure
is broad: only7/240 shirt and6/240 background frames meet the HF tolerance.
Those fixed-coordinate full-window counts include changing scene content and
are corroborating traces, not a new confirmatory gate.

The reviewer found quiet-HF mean error and quiet-MSE p95 regressions that pooled
quality improvements could hide. Parent retained them and inspected native
shirt/background stills. Neither party claimed playback.

The reviewer recommended testing the independent libaom policy next: the large
amplitude misses make a repeat45.7-minute preset calibration less informative
for feasibility. Parent accepted that recommendation while explicitly leaving
the matched-rate preset effect unresolved. It is not a claim that extra bits
cannot alter a result or that AV1 has reached a limit.
