## Completed broad screen and additional regression

The fixed 1,323-file source screen completed: 1,198 source comparisons, 107 weak
decoded overlays and 18 incomplete first-pass comparisons. Of the source
comparisons, 80 VC-1 samples require content-alignment review; no non-VC-1 sample
exceeded the separate coarse-picture review threshold. A lower score does not
certify perfect alignment. The 162 larger source-flat review candidates are
not a damage count or a replacement authorization. One sampled window cannot
clear a complete episode or film. The 1,474-version header snapshot also does
not cover the entire 5,293-file historical FGS inventory.

Nineteen stronger remaining candidates received six separated source/decoded
windows each. Fourteen episodes were explicitly selected from visible added
texture and recurrence: South Park S23E7, five Smiling Friends episodes, seven
Steven Universe episodes and The Owl House S2E7. Four more localized examples
remain documented without complete replacement. The apparent Steven Universe
S2E8 episode identity mismatch predates the ripple repair: the pre-ripple
backup already matches the retained Story for Steven source in the inspected
sequence. That file is excluded pending separate episode/import review; do not
mix another episode's video with its existing audio and subtitles.

`strong-tail-visual-reviews.json` retains the last fourteen decisions and five
exclusions. `remediation-strong-tail-verification.json`, complete displayed-header
scans and `installed-strong-tail-residuals.json` track the new versions. All
replacements preserve originals and superseded files; none of these selections
requires a download. Read current receipts before assuming a job has finished.
