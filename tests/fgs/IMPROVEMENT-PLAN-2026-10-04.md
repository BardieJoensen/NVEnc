### Closed or unsupported leads

Do not reopen encoder-knob sweeps, chroma bypass or post-quantization amplitude calibration without new current-production evidence. The flow already uses lookahead 32/level 3. The historical chroma bypass increased size while losing both colour planes; the alleged curve bias disappeared with occupancy weighting. The old Planet Earth separator defect was an alignment error against a library transcode. Agreement between NVEnc and libaom on coarse film is not proof of a format ceiling, and the coefficient optimizer's descriptor gains worsened diagonal structure.
